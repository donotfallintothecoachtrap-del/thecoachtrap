The Coach Trap community exists because most coaching advice online is either surface-level or designed to sell you something. This is neither.

It's a free Skool community founded by Greg Styan for coaches who are serious about building a sustainable business — getting the right clients, pricing properly, creating content that works, and building something that doesn't collapse the moment you take a week off.

Coaches at every stage are welcome: whether you're working towards your first client or refining a business that's already generating consistent income. The conversation is practical, the feedback is honest, and it costs nothing to join.
